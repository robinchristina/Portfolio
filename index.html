About me

Where counseling meets code

I spent five years as a school counselor — working with hundreds of students through grief, anxiety, conflict, learning differences, and everything in between. That work taught me something that no bootcamp can: how people actually feel when a tool fails them.

In 2020 I began learning to build for the web, studying alongside my husband and growing my skills through freeCodeCamp, personal projects, and now real client work at Remedy Web. I build things in React, Shopify, and vanilla JavaScript — with a counselor's instinct for what users actually need.

I'm also a homeschooling mother of five who has spent four years planning and teaching Waldorf-inspired curriculum. I understand schools, families, and classrooms from the inside — which is exactly the perspective EdTech needs more of.
